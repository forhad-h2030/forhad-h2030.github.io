Md Forhad Hossain

Experimental Physicist specializing in machine learning, data analysis, and detector systems for large-scale nuclear physics experiments

I'm Forhad, a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Virginia working on the SpinQuest (E1039) and SeaQuest (E906) experiments at Fermilab. My research targets the transverse single-spin asymmetry in Drell–Yan and J/ψ production to probe the spin structure of the nucleon.

Over eight years I have combined detector hardware — polarized cryogenic targets, NMR polarimetry, trigger commissioning — with machine learning and agentic AI to solve large-scale data analysis problems. I currently serve as Run and Analysis Coordinator for SpinQuest. Feel free to reach me at .

Selected Projects

SpinQuest Agentic MC Tuner

An LLM-driven agentic AI system (Ollama/llama3, ReAct loop) that autonomously tunes combinatoric background MC to match experimental data for the SpinQuest Drell–Yan experiment. Achieves KS = 0.043 via two-phase normalizing flow & BDT reweighting.

  • LLM agent
  • normalizing flows
  • pytorch

Transformer Dimuon Event Classifier

A feature-tokenization Transformer classifying SpinQuest dimuon events (J/ψ, ψ′, Drell–Yan, combinatoric) from 18 kinematic features. Self-attention maps expose physically interpretable feature correlations — invariant mass, opening angle, station tracking — providing interpretability the DNN baseline lacks.

  • pytorch
  • Transformer
  • attention maps

SpinQuest Signal and Background Classifier

An iterative multi-model DNN pipeline for SpinQuest dimuon classification. Binary DNNs sequentially classify J/ψ, ψ′, Drell–Yan, and combinatoric background events; MC simulation is tuned to experimental data (see Agentic MC Tuner); a final 4-class softmax DNN performs simultaneous multiclass classification.

  • pytorch
  • DNNs
  • multiclass classification

RUS — Fun4All I/O Interface for E1039

A C++ interface integrating a dependency-free ROOT-based I/O format (RUS) into the Fun4All framework. Implements input and output managers so users can register RUS files directly in Fun4All macros to run reconstruction or extract events. Used by QTracker, an ML-based muon track reconstruction pipeline for SpinQuest.

Experimental Collaborations

SpinQuest (E1039) — Fermilab

Fixed-target Drell–Yan experiment measuring the transverse single-spin asymmetry (TSSA) to extract the Sivers function of sea quarks, using a 120 GeV polarized proton beam with NH₃/ND₃ cryogenic targets at Fermilab.

SeaQuest (E906) — Fermilab

Fixed-target Drell–Yan experiment measuring the cross-section ratio p+d/p+p to determine the antiquark flavor asymmetry d̅/ū in the proton sea at Fermilab.
