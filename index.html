Md Forhad Hossain

Postdoctoral Researcher — Nuclear Physics & Machine Learning

Hello! I'm Forhad, a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Physics at the University of Virginia, working on the SpinQuest (E1039) and SeaQuest (E906) experiments at Fermilab. My research focuses on understanding the spin structure of the nucleon through fixed-target Drell–Yan and charmonium measurements.

I have over eight years of experience bridging hands-on detector work — polarized cryogenic targets, NMR polarimetry, trigger system commissioning — with modern data-analysis methods including machine learning (normalizing flows, deep neural networks) and Bayesian unfolding. I currently serve as Run and Analysis Coordinator for SpinQuest. Feel free to download my CV or reach me at !

Selected Projects

TrackPairGen — Normalizing Flow Event Generator

A combinatoric background event generator for SpinQuest (C++/Python, Fun4All). Produces track-pair events from Normalizing Flow-learned muon momentum distributions or uniform random distributions, with full detector simulation on Rivanna HPC and FermiGrid.

  • python 3
  • C++
  • normalizing flows

SpinQuest Combinatoric Background Classifier

A multi-model DNN pipeline for SpinQuest dimuon classification. Binary DNNs classify J/ψ, ψ′, Drell–Yan, and combinatoric background events; per-class Normalizing Flows tune simulations to data; a final 4-class softmax DNN performs simultaneous classification.

  • pytorch
  • DNNs
  • normalizing flows

SpinQuest Agentic MC Tuner

An LLM-driven agentic AI system (Ollama/llama3, ReAct loop) that autonomously tunes combinatoric background MC to match experimental data for the SpinQuest Drell–Yan experiment. Achieves KS = 0.043 via two-phase normalizing flow & BDT reweighting.

  • LLM agent
  • normalizing flows
  • pytorch
